PAWdLI Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about PAWdLI services, features, accounts, safety, and app support.

General PAWdLI FAQs
1. What is PAWdLI?

PAWdLI is a digital ecosystem created for pet parents, pet lovers, and pet businesses. It brings together pet entertainment, knowledge, adoption, veterinary support, pet connections, therapy, pet shopping, and dignified farewell services in one platform.

2. Who can use PAWdLI?

PAWdLI is designed for:

  • Pet parents
  • Pet lovers
  • People planning to adopt a pet
  • Pet businesses
  • Veterinarians and pet experts
  • Animal welfare organizations and shelters
3. Is PAWdLI only for dog owners?

No. PAWdLI is for all pet lovers and pet parents. Users can share and discover content related to dogs, cats, and other companion animals.

4. How do I create a PAWdLI account?

Download the PAWdLI app and complete the registration process using the information requested by the app.

5. Is PAWdLI free to use?

Many PAWdLI features are available to users without a subscription. Certain services, such as veterinary consultations or specific products or services, may involve charges.

6. How can I contact PAWdLI Support?

You can contact PAWdLI through the support or contact options available within the app or through the official PAWdLI support channels.

1. PAWdLI Pet Radio
1. What is Pet Radio?

Pet Radio is a live audio and video platform where pet parents, pet lovers, experts, and pet business owners can share stories, experiences, knowledge, and discussions related to pets.

2. Is Pet Radio live?

Yes. Pet Radio is designed for live sessions, allowing hosts and listeners to participate in real time.

3. Can I listen to Pet Radio without hosting a session?

Yes. Users can join available live sessions as listeners.

4. Who can host a Pet Radio session?

Pet parents, pet lovers, pet experts, and eligible pet business owners can participate as hosts, subject to PAWdLI's guidelines and approval process.

5. How long is a Pet Radio session?

Pet Radio sessions can be scheduled in available time slots, with sessions currently designed around one-hour slots.

6. Can I talk about my pet on Pet Radio?

Yes. Pet stories, experiences, care tips, grooming, training, awareness, and other relevant pet-related topics can be discussed.

7. Can pet businesses participate in Pet Radio?

Yes. Pet businesses can participate in relevant discussions and share useful information about their expertise and services without violating PAWdLI's content guidelines.

8. Can I earn by hosting Pet Radio?

Eligible hosts may have opportunities to receive payouts based on PAWdLI's applicable host and content policies.

2. PAWdLI Pet Podcast
1. What is Pet Podcast?

Pet Podcast is PAWdLI's recorded content platform featuring pet parents, experts, veterinarians, and pet lovers sharing stories, experiences, advice, and knowledge.

2. Is Pet Podcast live?

No. Unlike Pet Radio, Pet Podcast primarily features recorded sessions that users can watch or listen to at their convenience.

3. What topics are covered?

Topics can include:

  • Pet care
  • Grooming
  • Nutrition
  • Training
  • Breed-specific information
  • Pet behaviour
  • Pet parenting experiences
  • Expert advice
4. Can I participate in a Pet Podcast?

Pet parents, experts, and other suitable participants may be invited or selected by PAWdLI based on the content and topic.

5. Can veterinarians appear on Pet Podcast?

Yes. Veterinarians and other qualified experts can share educational information and expert insights.

6. Can I suggest a topic or guest?

Yes. Users can share suggestions with PAWdLI through the available support or contact channels.

3. PAWdLI Pet Therapy
1. What is Pet Therapy?

PAWdLI Pet Therapy connects people with pets to create positive, engaging experiences that can help people relax, interact, and take a break from everyday stress.

2. Where can Pet Therapy be conducted?

Depending on the service arrangement, Pet Therapy activities may be organized at:

  • Corporate offices
  • Events
  • Birthday celebrations
  • Community gatherings
  • Special occasions
3. Who can request Pet Therapy?

Companies, event organizers, communities, families, and individuals can enquire about Pet Therapy services.

4. Is Pet Therapy only for corporate offices?

No. It can be arranged for different types of events and environments where pet interaction is appropriate.

5. Can I book Pet Therapy for a private event?

Yes, subject to availability, location, pet suitability, and service requirements.

6. Are the therapy pets trained and vaccinated?

PAWdLI will determine suitable pets and handlers based on the nature of the event and applicable safety requirements. The pet is vaccinated and certified.

4. PAWdLI Pet Adoption
1. What is Pet Adoption on PAWdLI?

Pet Adoption helps connect pets needing a home with people looking to adopt and provide them with a safe and loving environment.

2. Can I post a pet for adoption?

Yes. Eligible users can submit adoption requests or posts through the adoption feature.

3. Is adoption free?

Adoption posts can be listed free of charge, although individual adoption situations may involve donations or other arrangements.

4. Can shelters post pets for adoption?

Yes. Animal shelters and eligible organizations can use the platform to share suitable adoption requirements.

5. Can I adopt a pet through PAWdLI?

Yes. You can discover available adoption posts and contact the relevant person or organization according to the information provided.

6. Does PAWdLI guarantee the adoption?

No. PAWdLI provides a platform to help connect adopters and adoption providers. Users should independently verify the pet's condition, background, and suitability before completing an adoption.

7. What should I check before adopting?

Consider:

  • Pet health
  • Vaccination status
  • Age
  • Behaviour
  • Living environment
  • Ability to provide long-term care
  • Compatibility with your family
5. Good Bye Buddy / Pet Farewell
1. What is PAWdLI Pet Farewell?

Pet Farewell is one of PAWdLI's core initiatives, created to help ensure that deceased animals, including pets found on roadsides, are treated with dignity and receive appropriate final rites.

2. Why did PAWdLI create Pet Farewell?

PAWdLI was created with the belief that every pet deserves dignity, even in its final moments.

3. Can I report a deceased animal found on the road?

Yes. If you encounter a deceased pet or animal and need assistance, you can submit a request through the Pet Farewell feature, where available.

4. What information should I provide?

You may be asked to provide:

  • Photograph of the animal
  • Location
  • Map location
  • Nearby landmarks
  • Directions or other useful information
5. What happens after I submit a request?

The request is received by the PAWdLI team. Based on the information provided and service availability, the team can coordinate the necessary response.

6. Can I report an animal if I don't know who the owner is?

Yes. Pet Farewell is particularly intended to help when an animal is found without an identifiable owner.

7. Does PAWdLI guarantee immediate response?

Response depends on location, accessibility, availability, and the circumstances of each request. PAWdLI will make reasonable efforts to respond to eligible requests.

8. Is Pet Farewell only for pets?

The initiative is primarily focused on helping animals that need dignified final arrangements, including companion animals and animals found without their families.

9. Is there a charge for Pet Farewell?

No charges are applicable.

10. Can I track my Good Bye Buddy request?

Yes. You can check the status and details of your Good Bye Buddy request through the PAWdLI app.

11. Is Good Bye Buddy available in all locations?

No. It is only available in Bangalore.

Make A Buddy helps pet parents and pet lovers discover and connect with other people and pets who may share similar interests, breeds, experiences, or locations.

7. What should I do if someone behaves inappropriately?

7. Meet A Vet
1. What is Meet A Vet?

Meet A Vet allows pet parents to connect digitally with veterinarians for veterinary consultation and guidance.

2. Can I consult a veterinarian through PAWdLI?

Yes. Users can request a consultation with an available veterinarian through the feature.

3. Is Meet A Vet free?

Veterinary consultations may involve a consultation fee. The applicable charge should be displayed before the consultation is confirmed.

4. Can a vet diagnose my pet online?

A veterinarian can provide guidance based on the information available during a digital consultation. However, some conditions require a physical examination, diagnostic tests, or an in-person veterinary visit.

5. Can I use Meet A Vet for emergencies?

Digital consultation should not be relied upon for life-threatening emergencies. In an emergency, contact the nearest veterinary hospital or emergency veterinary service immediately.

6. Can I share my pet's reports with the veterinarian?

Where supported by the app, relevant photographs, videos, reports, prescriptions, and other information can help the veterinarian understand the situation.

7. Can I get a prescription through the consultation?

Any prescription or treatment recommendation is entirely at the discretion of the consulting veterinarian and subject to applicable veterinary regulations.

8. Does PAWdLI provide medical treatment?

PAWdLI provides a platform for connecting pet parents with veterinarians. Medical decisions and treatment are the responsibility of the consulting veterinarian.

9. What happens if I lose my internet connection during a consultation?

If you lose your internet connection during a consultation, you can rejoin the session, provided you rejoin within the available consultation time.

10. What happens if the consultation gets disconnected?

You can rejoin the consultation session, provided you rejoin within the available consultation time.

PAWdLI Pet Store is designed to bring useful pet products together in one place for pet parents and pet lovers.

10. How long does a refund take?

Refunds generally take 7 days to be processed.

App Support FAQs
1. The PAWdLI app is not opening. What should I do?

Try the following:

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Check whether an app update is available.
  4. Restart your phone.
  5. Contact PAWdLI Support if the problem continues.
2. A feature is not working. What information should I provide to Support?

Please provide the following through contact@ivyisark.com:

  • Your registered contact details
  • Feature name
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshot or screen recording, if possible
  • Device type
  • App version, if available
3. I am not receiving notifications. What should I do?

Check that:

  • Notifications are enabled for PAWdLI.
  • Your phone is connected to the internet.
  • Battery-saving restrictions are not blocking notifications.
  • The app is updated.
4. Can I use PAWdLI on multiple devices?

This depends on the current account and device policies. Use your registered credentials and follow the login process provided by the app.

5. How do I delete my PAWdLI account?

Use the account-management option available within the app or contact PAWdLI Support to request account deletion, subject to applicable policies and legal requirements.
